# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Fellow founders, startup operators, builders, tech leaders, angel investors, and readers of Willis's essays and reflections.

## Product Purpose

Personal digital garden, founder hub, and living archive for Willis Wee. It serves to:
- Share authentic, candid essays and reflections on company building, leadership, AI workflows, parenting, and life transitions.
- Maintain a living personal User Guide and operating principles for effective collaboration.
- Curate high-signal book recommendations with personal takeaways.
- Showcase active and past projects (Slothware Labs, TickerTown, Tech in Asia, Friends! Learning Center).
- Provide direct avenues to connect (Substack, LinkedIn, X, GitHub, Intro.co).

## Positioning

An unvarnished, reflective, and builder-centric founder space. Distinct from standard corporate portfolios or growth-hack blogs, it combines 16 years of hard-won founder experience (from 0 to YC to exit) with open reflection, radical candor, and a commitment to "building for joy" and kindness.

## Operating Context

- Single-author personal website deployed statically to GitHub Pages (`williswee.com`).
- Direct HTML/CSS authoring workflow; articles and book entries are added systematically following established templates documented in `README.md`.
- Read and visited across desktop and mobile devices by founders seeking actionable lessons or personal insights.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities:**
  - About page (`index.html`) featuring bio, current focus, and social links.
  - Work timeline (`work.html`) highlighting career milestones, funding history, and AI initiatives.
  - Thoughts archive (`thoughts/`) with 35+ standalone reflection essays.
  - User Guide (`guide.html`) outlining communication and management preferences, debugging methods, and personal philosophies.
  - Curated Book list (`books.html`) with interactive client-side category filtering and tags.
- **Constraints:**
  - Strictly zero-build Vanilla HTML and CSS for maximum speed, simplicity, and longevity.
  - No external build tools, bundlers, or heavy JavaScript dependencies.
  - High performance, minimal footprint, and clean semantic markup.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice:** Candid, thoughtful, humble yet ambitious, grounded, clear, direct, and gentle ("kindness is not weakness", "building for joy").
- **Visual Feel:** Minimalist, fast, uncluttered, typography-focused layout with subtle accents and dark mode support.
- **Assets:** Personal avatar (`avatar.png`), standard SVGs for platform icons.

## Evidence on Hand

- 35+ published thought essays in [`thoughts/`](thoughts/).
- Comprehensive personal management guide in [`guide.html`](guide.html).
- Curated library of 30+ categorized books with reviews in [`books.html`](books.html).
- Documented founder history and milestones in [`work.html`](work.html).

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity and Authenticity over Polish:** Content and reflections must remain honest, transparent, and grounded in ground truth rather than posturing.
2. **Speed, Simplicity, and Longevity:** Plain static web standards ensure zero dependency rot, near-instant load times, and effortless long-term maintainability.
3. **High Signal Density:** Every page, article, and book review delivers concise, high-value takeaways without unnecessary fluff or filler.
4. **Accessible and Lightweight:** Content is structured for seamless reading across all screen sizes and environments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic HTML5 structure throughout all surfaces.
- Accessible color contrast for both light and dark viewing contexts.
- Responsive design adapting gracefully to all viewport sizes.
