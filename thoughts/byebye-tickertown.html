We’re winding down TickerTown

Building the game was much more fun than running it afterwards.

By Willis Wee

23 November 2026

I had a lot of fun building TickerTown with Ian. Some of you kept playing and sharing feedback with us after we launched on 4 November. But we soon realized we didn’t want to keep running the game.

A few quick thoughts:

1) Onboarding seems okay

Out of the 54 players we tracked, 33 tried out a strategy with play money. That’s a 61% onboarding success rate.

I felt pretty good about that because we spent most of our testing time on onboarding, and it seems to have paid off. For comparison, the average for consumer freemium and subscription products was 40% in Lenny Rachitsky and Yuriy Timen’s survey.

2) TickerTown isn’t sticky

Between 16 and 22 November, we only had 16 weekly active users.

Some of you mentioned that logging back in with an email code was annoying. I agree. A few of you said you’d probably play more if there were a mobile app. I agree with that too. But making those changes would take a ton of work, and honestly, I just didn’t want to do it.

3) No founder fit

Once we launched TickerTown, Ian and I lost our motivation. Building the game was much more fun than running it afterwards.

For me, I only have a few hours to build things between dad duties. I’m not sure what I’ll make next, but I still want to build just for the fun of it.

Thanks for playing and for letting us know what worked and what didn’t. 🙏
