Dear students and fresh grads

Enjoy this period of stress, suffering, and growth. Seize the chance.

By Willis Wee

18 November 2026

Dear students and fresh grads,

I envy you and admire you.

You have energy, youth, and time on your side. Making a mistake is not a problem.

I had my best time as a university student, building without thinking about any consequence or judgment. That was one of the best stretches of building I’ve had. It ended up as a 16-year journey.1 No regrets. 11/10 will do it again.

I paid myself peanuts2 to protect my company’s resources, often working 18 hours a day, including the weekends. I could only work that hard because I was young, hungry, and somewhat naive and foolish.3

If you’re young and reading this, remember it will pass very quickly. You may never get back the same carefree feeling of building your hearts out. Enjoy this period of stress, suffering, and growth. Seize the chance.

Don’t get me wrong, I’m not salty.

Like I said, I’ve lived the last 16 years the best I could, with no regrets. I built whatever I could, and whatever I thought I potentially could. I’m glad to be where I am today. I can’t believe I turned out OKAY.4

That being said, I’m so used to building and hard work that it takes a while to actually slow down and dedicate time to my wife, 3 girls, and family.

After about a year of side projects and experiments, I logically concluded that family is the constraint I want. My kids deserve my time, and I’ll have to make do with the 16 hours per week I have for building.5

A few exited founder friends did tell me the same. I just didn’t have the wisdom to fully comprehend it, because I needed to walk my own journey to come to a similar awareness.

This is the only time my kids will grow up still depending on me, looking up to me, wanting to be around me, and not finding me old and boring af. So, I’m gonna seize it.

1 More here on what i did.

2 I paid myself $500 for the first few years. No regrets, but not something I’d recommend.

3 I did a lot of stupid and wrong things but usually quick to reflect, admit, apologize, and course correct.

4 Most of my extended family had written my brother and me off. Somehow, we did okay. Lucky.

5 Probably lesser counting in the school holidays.
