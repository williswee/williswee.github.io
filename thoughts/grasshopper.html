Daddy, what can you buy with $0.20?

My two older girls had never heard of $0.20 recess money. So I told them about the grasshoppers.

By Willis Wee

8 October 2026

It was a challenging morning with my two older girls. On the way to school, we ended up talking about hardship.

The conversation below is between my girls and me. It’s paraphrased from a short car ride.

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: What do you mean hardship? What is that?

Daddy 🧔🏻: You each get $2.501 for recess every day. That’s more than enough for canteen food. When I was your age, 大伯 (my older brother) and I each got $0.20.

👧🏻👧🏻: 20 cents?! What can you buy with $0.20

Daddy 🧔🏻: A soya bean drink. Maybe. It’s not enough for a bowl of noodles, which was at least $0.50. So we had to save for two or three days before we could eat a proper recess.

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: What did you do with the money then?

Daddy 🧔🏻: There was nothing to spend on. We didn’t have enough to buy food. Instead of thinking about what to spend, we started thinking about how to make more money.

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: How did you make more money?

Daddy 🧔🏻: Catch and sell grasshoppers.

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: Grasshoppers?!

Daddy 🧔🏻: We had nothing to do during recess. We’d be out in the grass fields, playing football or catching grasshoppers, because there were so many of them. We’d put them in a container and watch them. Then we noticed the other kids were curious. They wanted the grasshoppers as pets.

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: So you sold them?

Daddy 🧔🏻: Catching grasshoppers takes work. If other kids wanted them but didn’t want to do the catching, we could do it and sell them. We charged $0.50 up to $2, depending on the size and the type.2

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: That’s a lot! What happened?

Daddy 🧔🏻: We felt rich, haha. We actually earned it with our own hands. But the school discipline master eventually shut us down.3 The business was getting pretty big. More kids wanted them as pets.

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: And then?

Daddy 🧔🏻: With the grasshopper money, we could eat well and pay off our debts.

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: Debts?!

Daddy 🧔🏻: I used to borrow money from a friend whenever I was hungry. Thankfully, he was kind. His mother even found out and came to school once during recess to eat together with us, to make sure I actually had food. I’m still grateful for it.4

Girls 👧🏻👧🏻: How do you keep a grasshopper? How do you feed it? How do you clean it? How did you sell the grasshopper?

Daddy 🧔🏻: (laughs, hahaha) Go go, close the door properly. Bye bye, I love you. Have fun at school.

I don’t know how much of this they’ll remember.5

But if anything stays, I hope it’s this: you can use your own hands and your own thinking to work your way out of a challenging spot. There is always a way, as long as you stay aware, remain calm, and are willing to do the work.

1 Singapore dollar (SGD)

2 Blue legs = $1.00, Red legs = $1.50, Green legs = $0.50, Rare = $2.00 (i.e. giant size, chinese, tobacco grasshoppers). We expanded the biz to pray mantis, spiders, and more. But it didn’t last long.

3 I’m not a fan of this discipline master. I wasn’t a great student, but there was no need to treat a kid with disrespect. Once, he smacked a grasshopper out of my hand and killed it. There was no need to take a life.

4 I forgot his name. It’s “Wei” something. If you ever read this, i'd like to reconnect.

5 I think i told them this story before. But they forgot about it. 🤔
