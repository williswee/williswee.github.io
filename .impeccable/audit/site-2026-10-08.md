## Fixed: surrounding text became unreadable during discovery

**P1 — Books and Gratitude discovery states.** Selecting a random book or gratitude note reduced the opacity of the introduction and surrounding entries to `.22`. Their text remained visible and available to interact with, but the resulting contrast fell to approximately **1.50:1 for metadata** and **1.67:1 for body text** against the reading panel.

The shared `--reading-context-opacity` token is now `.7`. Books and Gratitude use it for surrounding text, producing approximately **5.07:1 for metadata** and **6.93:1 for body text** after compositing. Selected entries and focused content retain full opacity. Decorative landscape artwork retains its `.22` dimming, preserving the intended emphasis.

Relevant locations:

- `thoughts/reading-room.css:14` — shared context-opacity token.
- `books-game.css:56` and `:58` — surrounding book cards and introduction.
- `gratitude-game.css:25` and `:27` — surrounding notes and introduction.
- `scripts/tests/focus-appearance.test.mjs:39` — regression check for composited text contrast.
- `DESIGN.md` — documented shared interaction rule.

Desktop Books verification covered random selection and keyboard recovery: the Top control clears the selection and returns focus to the random picker. The selected entry, surrounding entries, and page framing remain visually distinct without sacrificing readability.

The final Gratitude check at **390×844** confirmed that selected note #29 is fully visible, adjacent notes #30 and #28 have computed opacity `.7`, and the page has no horizontal overflow or observed console errors. The updated shared and page stylesheets loaded successfully. Desktop Books likewise confirmed `.7` opacity on neighboring entries and no horizontal overflow.

## Performance and shared implementation

The six homepage artwork files total **1,940,204 bytes**. The first image has high fetch priority; later scenes are deliberately eager with low fetch priority to support the established Safari behavior. This pass found no evidence warranting a change to that policy.

Images have explicit dimensions and asynchronous decoding. The reviewed media does not autoplay. Reduced-motion handling is present in the shared interactions. No art regeneration, asset resizing, or content rewriting was needed for this polish pass.

## Detector review

The deterministic Impeccable scan returned **13 matches: 12 advisories and one copy warning**. It ran in degraded regex mode because parser dependencies were unavailable. Selector-aware analysis, custom-property resolution, and computed contrast checks were therefore not provided by that scan. Browser checks and the focused contrast regression supply the relevant verification for the implemented fix.

Reviewed matches were dismissed as existing, intentional choices: responsive display and logotype sizes, documented reading and dialogue typography, translucent ornament and shadows, the Work divider color, and the homepage navigation color. The Gratitude prose warning concerns the user's factual writing; changing that writing to satisfy a style detector would be inappropriate. No additional defects were inferred from these matches.
