# Earth texture

`earth-night.jpg` is NASA's **Black Marble 2016** global night-lights
composite, at its original 3600 × 1800 resolution. It is a static historical
texture, not a live nighttime view.

- Source: [NASA Earth Observatory — Earth at Night / Black Marble: Flat Maps](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/)
- Original file: [BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg)
- Credit: NASA Earth Observatory / NOAA Suomi NPP VIIRS.
- [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)

  copyright in the United States and may be used for informational web pages
  with acknowledgement. This use does not imply NASA endorsement.
