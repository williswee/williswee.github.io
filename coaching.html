Who it’s for

Founders going from 0 to 1, or 1 to 2. You’re probably the top person in the company, and there aren’t many people you can think out loud with.

I’m probably not a fit if you’re looking for someone to make the decisions for you.

What we can work on

  • 1 Getting clarity on your ideas, thoughts, or path. Mission-driven founders
  • 2 Going deep into root causes. My debugging process
  • 3 Building an MVP smartly and cheaply. The boring ways I use AI
  • 4 Getting your first customers. A founder asked me to review her user interview Qs. Here’s what I told her.
  • 5 Product, design, GTM and biz dev. What I did at TIA
  • 6 Leading people through hard stretches. There’s no such thing as a perfect layoff · A letter to founders who are burning out

How it works

🎒 What should I bring?

A challenge, the context, and what you’ve tried. Missing data, too many paths, strong emotions, or a skill you haven’t learned yet. All is good.

🤝 What does “comrades” mean?

We work through challenges together, on equal footing. I’m friendly and candid, and open to your feedback too. More about me in my user guide.

🧭 What will I leave with?

A clearer sense of your options and where to go next. I can share concrete examples or help draft a plan. You own the decisions and execution.

A bit about me

I made a lot of mistakes along the way at Tech in Asia. If I did it all again, I’d do it just kinder.

These days I’m building small things at Slothware Labs (opens in a new tab), angel investing a little, and spending lots of time at home with my 3 kiddos.

After my 9-day meditation retreat, a few things got clearer. One of them: I really enjoy helping founders get unstuck. I didn’t use to. Now it sparks joy.
