Who it’s for

At the start, you may be figuring out what to build, how to test it, or how to get your first customers. Once the business is running, the questions may be about product direction, reaching customers, or leading your team.

You're probably the top person in the company, and there aren't many people you can think out loud with.

I’m probably not a fit if you’re looking for someone to make the decisions for you.

What we can work on

  • 1 Deciding which idea or direction to pursue. Mission-driven founders
  • 2 Finding the root cause of a problem. My debugging process
  • 3 Building a first version of your product without overspending. The boring ways I use AI
  • 4 Getting your first customers. A founder asked me to review her user interview Qs. Read my notes on user interviews and usability tests.
  • 5 Product, design, go-to-market plans, and business development. What I did at TIA

How it works

Can we meet online, and what does it cost?

You can book a one-to-one video call through Intro (opens in a new tab). The booking page lists current session lengths, rates, and available times.

🎒 What should I bring?

A challenge, the context, and what you’ve tried. Missing data, too many paths, strong emotions, or a skill you haven’t learned yet. All is good.

🤝 What does “comrades” mean?

We work through challenges together, on equal footing. I’m friendly and candid, and open to your feedback too. More about me in my user guide.

🧭 What will I leave with?

A clearer sense of your options and where to go next. I can share concrete examples or help draft a plan. You own the decisions and execution.
